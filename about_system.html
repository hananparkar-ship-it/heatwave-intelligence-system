About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The portal brings together regional heat guidance, safety information and interactive tools for reviewing readings that visitors enter. It does not monitor live weather or issue official warnings.

Data Sources

Operational heat monitoring can combine Automatic Weather Station readings, IMD forecasts, satellite observations and other weather sensors.

Stakeholders

Meteorologists, disaster management teams, public health services and residents use weather information to plan precautions and respond to heat risks.

How to use the portal

Review the alert guidance, compare regional heat-risk factors and use the hotspot map to open a zone summary. The forecast page explains which local conditions to consider. For current warnings and observations, use official weather updates.

Regional coverage

The portal groups locations into North, Northeast, West, Central and South zones. The Northeast has its own regional summary because humidity and monsoon rainfall can shape heat stress alongside temperature. No zone has a fixed risk level; local conditions and forecasts determine risk.

From observations to public guidance

Weather observations can be reviewed alongside forecasts and local conditions. Clear messages help residents, outdoor workers, healthcare teams and emergency planners decide when to take precautions and where to find additional support.

Timely weather information helps communities prepare for periods of extreme heat and choose appropriate precautions.
