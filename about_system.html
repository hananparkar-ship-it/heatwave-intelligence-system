About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The Heatwave Intelligence System helps monitor temperature, identify heatwave conditions and provide early warnings to protect people from extreme weather.

Data Sources

Information is collected from AWS stations, IMD records, satellite observations and weather sensors.

Stakeholders

Meteorologists, disaster management officers, government authorities and citizens use this system for weather monitoring and safety planning.


How to use the portal

Start with the alert bulletin for the current example warning, then compare the regional forecast and hotspot map. The zone pages provide a short summary and practical reminders for each area.

Understanding the demonstration

This is a student demonstration of a climate information portal. Its displayed temperatures and warning levels are example content rather than a live forecast. For decisions about current conditions, check the latest official meteorological and local authority updates.

From observations to public guidance

Weather observations can be reviewed alongside forecasts and local conditions. Clear messages help residents, outdoor workers, healthcare teams and emergency planners decide when to take precautions and where to find additional support.
