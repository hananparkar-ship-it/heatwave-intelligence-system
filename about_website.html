About this website

About Heatwave Intelligence

Heatwave Intelligence is a website project about heatwave prediction, public awareness and practical safety. It brings climate context, heat-risk guidance and useful links together in one place.

The purpose

The site explains how heatwave assessments consider local maximum temperatures, departures from normal, the duration of hot conditions and factors such as humidity and overnight recovery. Its six climate-class profiles provide broad background context for understanding why heat impacts can differ across locations.

What you can explore

  • Climate-class profiles and the interactive hotspot map
  • Heatwave alerts, safety advice and official information links
  • Browser-based tools for reviewing values entered by the user
  • Media and guidance for heat safety

About the information

This is an educational website demonstration. It does not operate weather sensors or other hardware, publish live observations, issue official warnings or generate its own regional prediction model. Dated outlooks from official sources may be shown with their issue date and source link. Values entered into the interactive tools are used only for the on-page demonstration. The subscription form does not store details or send alerts.

For current weather, heatwave predictions and official warnings, check the India Meteorological Department.
