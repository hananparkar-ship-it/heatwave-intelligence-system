Heatwave Alert Bulletin

Alert: Severe Heatwave Warning

Region: North Zone

Citizens are advised to follow heat safety precautions and avoid unnecessary outdoor activities.

Maximum Temperature : 46oC

Bulletin Version : 2.1

Example Scenario Date : 15th June 2026

AWS, IMD, HW, SHW data are used for monitoring.

How to use this bulletin

Check the named region and maximum temperature, then open the hotspot map or regional forecast for more context. Conditions can vary between nearby locations, so follow the latest local announcements when planning outdoor work or travel.

Heat safety reminders

Drink water regularly, choose shaded or cooler places when possible, and limit strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day. Check on people who may need extra support, and contact local emergency services if someone appears seriously unwell.
