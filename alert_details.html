What this alert means

No location-specific alert level or maximum temperature is published here, so this page cannot confirm whether a heatwave warning is active in your area. Use the current IMD bulletin for the official region, issue date and valid period.

What should you do?

Stay cool

Use shade or a cooler place and plan outdoor activity for cooler hours.

Stay hydrated

Drink water regularly and follow advice from your healthcare provider.

Limit outdoor exposure

Avoid unnecessary time in direct sun and take breaks if you must work outdoors.

Check on others

Contact older adults, young children and anyone who may need extra support.

Urgent help

Emergency information

Confusion, collapse or severe weakness during extreme heat may require urgent medical care. Move the person to a cooler place and get help.

Call Disaster Helpline 1078
