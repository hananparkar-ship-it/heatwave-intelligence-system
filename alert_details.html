Heatwave Alert Bulletin

Review practical precautions and use official sources for current warning levels and affected areas.

Current warnings: Check the India Meteorological Department for official updates. This page provides general safety guidance.

Advisory

Drink water regularly, use shaded or cooler places when possible, and limit strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day. Check on people who may need extra support. Seek urgent medical help if someone becomes confused, collapses or appears seriously unwell.

How to use this bulletin

  1. Check the issuing authority, region and bulletin time.
  2. Follow the listed precautions and recheck for updated advice.
  3. Use local authority guidance for travel, work and emergency decisions.
