Regional profile

Central Zone

This regional grouping covers Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha and West Bengal.

Heat-risk context

Consider daily maximum temperatures by location and season, paying attention to persistent hot periods and regional differences. Inland areas and individual locations can experience different conditions, so a zone label alone cannot assign severity. Local observations help assess conditions for a particular place.

Safety guidance

Drink water regularly, use shaded or cooler places during peak heat and follow local advisories for outdoor work and travel.

This broad regional summary is not a current reading or a fixed heat-risk classification. Conditions vary between locations; use official IMD updates for current observations and warnings.
