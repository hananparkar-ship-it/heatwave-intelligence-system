Climate class profile

Arid

Northwestern drylands, including parts of Rajasthan and adjoining areas.

Climate characteristics

Very limited rainfall and hot, dry periods are characteristic of this climate class. Local conditions vary by place and season.

Heatwave context

Assess heat using location-specific maximum temperatures, departure from the local normal and duration. This climate label alone does not indicate a current heatwave or risk level.

This is a general climate profile, not a live observation, forecast or active warning. For current local conditions, check official India Meteorological Department updates.
