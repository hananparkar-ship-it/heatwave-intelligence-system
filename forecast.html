General Climate Class

Arid

Hot & Dry
  • Hot, dry periods
  • Low rainfall
  • Strong daytime heating
  • Persistent seasonal heat

Climate characteristics

A dry climate class associated with limited rainfall and hot conditions. This general profile does not describe current weather at a particular location.

Prediction Factors

  • Daily maximum temperature
  • Departure from the local normal
  • Humidity and resulting heat stress
  • Duration and persistence of hot conditions
  • Nighttime minimum temperature and overnight recovery
  • Forecast trend
  • Rainfall, cloud cover and monsoon context where relevant

Climate-class factors to consider: Maximum temperature, departure from normal, persistence and overnight recovery.

Heatwave Classification

IMD heatwave classification is based primarily on station maximum temperature and its departure from normal, with different context for plains, hills and coastal stations. Humidity can increase heat stress, but it is not by itself an official heatwave classification criterion. See the IMD criteria.

Heat Stress

How people experience heat can also depend on humidity, duration, wind, sunshine, activity and whether nights provide relief. These factors inform public-health guidance and should be considered alongside—not substituted for—formal heatwave classification.
