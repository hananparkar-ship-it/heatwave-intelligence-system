Understand the readings

Heatwave Monitoring

In an operational climate service, temperature, humidity and forecast information can be reviewed together to identify possible heat risks.

This demonstration shows how a portal can organize those details so they are easier to understand.

Regional summaries and hotspot views help residents and planning teams compare example conditions.

For current readings and decisions, refer to the latest official weather updates for your location.

02

Know when risk rises

Heatwave Alerts

An operational alert service can share the expected conditions, affected areas and recommended precautions when heat risk increases.

The bulletin linked here is a sample layout and does not send notifications.

Early warnings allow people to plan their daily activities, avoid unnecessary exposure to extreme heat and take appropriate safety measures.

Authorities can also use these alerts to respond quickly to heatwave conditions.

03

Explore affected areas

Hotspot Information

The system identifies areas experiencing unusually high temperatures and provides information about affected locations.

This helps authorities focus their resources on regions that require immediate attention.

Hotspot information can also help citizens understand which areas are experiencing extreme conditions and take suitable precautions before travelling, working outdoors or participating in outdoor activities.

04

Reduce heat exposure

Safety Advisory

During a heatwave, people should drink plenty of water and avoid unnecessary outdoor activities during peak afternoon hours.

Staying indoors or in shaded areas can reduce exposure to extreme heat.

People should wear light and comfortable clothing, take regular breaks when working outdoors and keep their homes properly ventilated during periods of high temperature.

05

Get help quickly

Emergency Information

In case of heat-related illness, medical assistance should be sought immediately.

Warning signs may include dizziness, weakness, excessive sweating and confusion.

People should move to a cooler location and avoid further exposure to heat.

Elderly individuals, children and people working outdoors should be monitored carefully during extreme heat events.
