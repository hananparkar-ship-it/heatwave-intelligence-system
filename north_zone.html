Regional profile

North Zone

This regional grouping covers Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Uttarakhand, Delhi and Uttar Pradesh.

Heat-risk context

Review local temperature, humidity, heat duration and overnight recovery. Conditions vary across the plains, hills and cities.

Safety guidance

Drink water regularly, seek shade or a cooler place during peak heat, and limit strenuous outdoor activity when official advisories recommend it.

Risk level should be based on current local observations and forecasts, not on the region name alone. Check official updates before making weather-related decisions.
