Regional profile

Northeast Zone

This distinct regional grouping covers Sikkim and the seven northeastern states: Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura.

Heat-risk context

Assess daily maximum-temperature patterns by location and season alongside humidity and monsoon conditions. Heat stress can vary between humid valleys and higher-elevation locations, so a single zone-wide value would be misleading. Local observations help describe conditions for a particular place.

Safety guidance

Drink water regularly, use a cooler or shaded place when possible, and follow local advisories during hot and humid periods.

This broad regional summary does not show current readings or a fixed heat-risk classification. Conditions can vary between hill and valley locations; use official IMD updates for current observations and warnings.

For current observations, heatwave predictions and official warnings, check the India Meteorological Department.
