Reference and guidance

Resources

Use trusted meteorological and public-health sources to understand heat conditions, official warnings and recommended precautions. This website provides reference information; it does not collect weather readings or issue forecasts.

Data Sources

  • Historical IMD maximum-temperature records support regional and seasonal heatwave analysis. The IMD Pune repository is a source for climate data; this website does not republish its records.
  • Official weather observations and IMD forecasts help describe local conditions and provide the basis for current weather and heatwave updates.
  • Satellite observations can add broad spatial context to weather and climate analysis.

System Workflow

A public heatwave information service can organize its work into these steps:

  1. Access weather information from official sources.
  2. Analyze maximum-temperature records by location, time, region and season.
  3. Assess heatwave indicators using maximum temperature and departure from normal.
  4. Review forecast information and warning criteria.
  5. Share clear public-safety advice for the affected place and valid period.
