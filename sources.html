Data Sources and Stakeholders

Heat monitoring can draw on weather-station observations, forecasts and satellite data. Sources update at different times and may report conditions at different scales, so check the issuing organization and publication time.

This portal links to official resources but does not ingest current observations or issue official warnings. Use the source pages below for current conditions.
