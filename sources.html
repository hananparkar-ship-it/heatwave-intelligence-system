Reference and guidance

Resources

Heat monitoring uses observations, forecasts and satellite information. Sources update at different times and cover different areas, so check the issuing organization and publication time. This portal links to official resources but does not ingest current observations or issue official warnings. Use the source pages below for current conditions.
