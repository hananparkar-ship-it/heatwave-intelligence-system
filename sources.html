Data Sources and Stakeholders

Climate information systems bring observations and forecasts together so people can see what is happening, where conditions may worsen, and what guidance is available. Different sources update at different times and may report conditions at different scales.

Use the source organization and issue time when reviewing a forecast. This student portal uses illustrative content; consult official advisories for current warnings.
