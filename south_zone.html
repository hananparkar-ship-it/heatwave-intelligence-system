Regional profile

South Zone

This regional grouping covers Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

Heat-risk context

Compare daily maximum-temperature patterns by location and season, and account for differences between coastal, inland and elevated areas. Humidity and monsoon conditions can shape local heat stress, so local conditions matter.

Safety guidance

Stay hydrated, take breaks in cooler places and reduce strenuous outdoor activity during peak heat, following local advisories.

This broad regional summary is not a current reading or a fixed heat-risk classification. Conditions vary between locations; check official IMD updates for current observations and warnings.
