Regional profile

West Zone

This regional grouping covers Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Goa.

Heat-risk context

Conditions can differ between inland, desert, urban and coastal locations. Review temperature, humidity and the local forecast together.

Safety guidance

Stay hydrated, seek shade or a cooler place during peak heat and follow local advisories for outdoor work and travel.

Risk level depends on current local observations and forecast conditions, not on the zone name alone.

For current forecasts and warnings, check the official issuing authority before making weather-related decisions.
